# PRODUCT_DNA

## What Makes Us Different

The **ProductDNA** describes our core product philosophy, capabilities, and evolution.

### Core Capabilities

- **AI-Powered Simplicity** – Built-in AI assistants guide users through complex tasks with minimal friction.
- **Modular Architecture** – Components are loosely coupled, allowing independent scaling and updates.
- **Real-Time Analytics** – Live dashboards and insights keep teams aligned on performance.
- **Extensible Ecosystem** – Plug-and-play modules for ads, affiliates, and commerce.

### Evolution

1. **Foundation** (Q3 2026) – Basic landing pages and product catalogs
2. **Enhancement** (Oct 2026) – AI agents, affiliate systems, and ad management
3. **Scale** (Q4 2026) – Multi-platform deployment, edge functions, and enterprise integration

### Key Features

- **AI Agents Module** – OpenRouter-supported autonomous agents for task execution.
- **Affiliate Conversation Scripts** – Automated merchant-center integrations.
- **Advertising Suite** – Google Ads preview, column mapping, and single CTA optimization.
- **Merchant API** – Supabase Edge Functions for developer registration and status.
- **SEO & Visibility** – Optimized tooltips, breadcrumb labels, and heading standards.
