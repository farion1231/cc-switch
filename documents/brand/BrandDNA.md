# BrandDNA

## Core Identity

The **BrandDNA** defines our visual and verbal identity, ensuring consistency across all touchpoints.

### Visual Identity
- **Primary Color**: #2563EB (Blue) – conveys trust and technology
- **Secondary Color**: #F59E0B (Amber) – adds warmth and energy
- **Typography**: 
  - Headlines: Bold sans-serif (e.g., Inter, 24px)
  - Body: Regular sans-serif (e.g., Inter, 16px)
- **Logo Usage**: Minimalist icon + wordmark, scalable vector format

### Verbal Identity
- **Voice**: Professional, approachable, and confident
- **Tone**: Clear, direct, and solution-oriented
- **Key Principles**:
  - Be helpful, not salesy
  - Focus on outcomes, not features
  - Use active voice and concrete language

### Design System
- **Grid**: 12-column, 8px gutter
- **Spacing**: 4px (small), 8px (medium), 16px (large)
- **Components**: Consistent button styles, form layouts, and card designs
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA compliance, sufficient contrast ratios

## Brand Pillars

1. **Innovation** – Leading-edge AI solutions for e-commerce
2. **Simplicity** – Intuitive interfaces that reduce friction
3. **Reliability** – Robust infrastructure with real-time analytics
4. **Scalability** – Designed for growth and global reach

## Application

- **Website**: Header logo, footer branding, CTAs
- **Marketing Materials**: Social posts, email templates, presentations
- **Internal**: Slides, documentation, support guidelines
- **Mobile**: App icons, onboarding screens
